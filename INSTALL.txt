Match Your Colors v1.3
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.

Recolors your own helmet to match your armor, or your armor to match your
helmet (Mod Options Menu, MODS tab: Color Matching), or paints both in one of
the game's weapon paint schemes (Paint Scheme). Visors, lenses, lights and
glowing parts keep their colors. Toggles: Leave Complete Sets, Recolor Hoods
(off keeps a hood's own color), Match Materials (on paints metal parts that
take a painted color), Recolor Cape (on: your cape takes the same colors).
Squadmates who use this mod (1.3 or newer) see each other's colors (Sync With
Mod Users, always on: your settings are stored in the squad's online lobby);
players without it see your gear as it is. Helmets and armors changed by LUT
mods are recolored from their modded colors.

Log: %LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/MatchYourColors.log
